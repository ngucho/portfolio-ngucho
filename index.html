About

        

        

          

            
Data Scientist & AI Project Lead with 4+ years designing, deploying and governing Data/AI solutions end to end — and an entrepreneur who co-founded two startups.

            
At Safran Aircraft Engines I lead a team of four engineers and own an architecture that goes from high-frequency sensor data to real-time anomaly detection, digital twins and the dashboards people actually use. Models ship through CI/CD and are monitored around the clock.

            
Outside Safran I build products where AI meets real needs: Docfira's Scribe drafts a full consultation report in about 30 seconds; at Scoop Afrique I bring speech AI to a media watched 300M+ times a month; and I'm building Zephyru, a dating app driven by matching algorithms.

            
My background — theoretical physics, applied mathematics, mechanics — means I like to understand the system before modelling it, then measure what the model changes.
